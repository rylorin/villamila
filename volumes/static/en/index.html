ABOUT VILLA MILA

ARONIA IS LIFE, ARONIA IS OUR PASSION

OUR PRODUCTS

Our Villa Mila premium product line is made from our own aronia. We produce top quality aronia from our fields grown using ecological products only. We using selected berries and first juice only to bring to your home all the exceptional qualities of aronia. Our speciality is an aronia wine that will bring a healthy drink on your table.

OUR TEAM

We are a family team that grows its own aronia fields. Our products are made under control of Ivana, the owner of the fields, who make our aonia wine herself. Other members of the family contribute as well as local farmers. We are devoted to manual work using ecological only materials and fair trade.
